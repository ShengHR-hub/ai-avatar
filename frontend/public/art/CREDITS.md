## Notes on processing

Every portrait was downloaded from the Unsplash image CDN at a square 1:1 aspect ratio, framed with Unsplash's face-aware crop (`fit=facearea`), and exported at two sizes: `*-640.jpg` (640×640) and `*-1200.jpg` (1200×1200). The face is horizontally centred in each square, so a later centre-crop to 4:5 keeps the whole head inside the frame. Each 640×640 variant was downscaled from its 1200×1200 counterpart. No other edits were applied.
